# Fixed Intro Style

## Identity

The channel identity is practical and technical: AI is shown as an assistant layer on top of established tools, not as a replacement for them. The opener should feel like a polished workflow note before a hands-on tutorial.

## Visual System

- Background: dark, slightly blurred source-video still or dark generated workstation field.
- Grid: thin cyan blueprint lines with low opacity and slow parallax.
- Geometry: animated CAD-style linework, arcs, circles, and construction marks.
- Accent: amber transition line and small active status marks.
- Typography: bold Chinese title with SimHei or Microsoft YaHei; compact subtitle below.

## Camera And Timing

- 0.00-0.35s: dark workstation background fades in with grid motion.
- 0.35-0.90s: CAD linework reveals and scan line crosses the frame.
- 0.85-1.65s: main title fades/slides into a locked center-left position.
- 1.65-2.75s: subtitle and small tags settle while background keeps pushing in.
- 2.75-3.20s: amber transition bar sweeps across, preparing the cut into the real recording.

## Default Text

- Title: `AI + 传统工具`
- Subtitle: `自动绘图 / 攻略 / 实战分享`
- Caption: `WORKFLOW NOTES`

## Output Convention

Prefer:

- `outputs/video_intro/<source-stem>_intro.mp4`
- `outputs/video_intro/<source-stem>_with_intro.mp4`
